Create a short seamless cinematic loop for the VK lifestyle community «Вещи Кстати».

BRAND STYLE

«Вещи Кстати» is a calm editorial lifestyle brand about beautiful and useful everyday objects.

The visual style is:

warm editorial catalog;
premium but approachable;
minimal;
soft;
natural;
slightly tactile;
with a subtle origami-inspired identity.

The scene must NOT look like a marketplace advertisement.

It should feel like a beautifully art-directed lifestyle magazine photograph that has quietly come to life.

COLOR PALETTE

Warm ivory.
Cream.
Soft beige.
Taupe.
Muted sage.
Olive.
Warm graphite.
Natural wood tones.
Very small subtle gold accents.

Avoid:

bright red;
neon;
acid colors;
strong blue;
cheap saturated colors;
aggressive contrast.

MATERIALS

Use realistic tactile materials:

ceramic;
linen;
cotton;
wood;
matte metal;
paper;
glass;
stone;
matte plastic when appropriate.

Textures should feel real and premium.

LIGHTING

Soft natural daylight.

Prefer:
window light;
late morning light;
soft evening light;
gentle warm shadows.

No dramatic studio spotlights.

No glossy commercial lighting.

No harsh reflections.

COMPOSITION

Clean editorial still life.

Lots of negative space.

One main object or a small group of 2–4 related objects.

Balanced asymmetry.

Objects should look naturally placed, not arranged like a marketplace product grid.

The image may include:

paper folds;
thin golden line;
small origami bird or abstract origami detail;
subtle circular editorial mark;
soft textile;
small branch or leaf;
neutral interior fragment.

Origami elements must remain subtle.

They are a brand detail, not the main subject.

ANIMATION

The movement must be very restrained.

The GIF should feel almost like a photograph.

Animate only one or two details.

Good movements:

soft curtain movement;
a plant leaf moving slightly;
steam slowly rising from a cup;
sunlight shifting gently;
a linen cloth moving from a faint breeze;
a paper edge lifting slightly;
small reflection moving across glass;
candle flame moving subtly;
a slow hand movement if a person is included;
a small object being gently placed on a surface.

Avoid excessive object movement.

Do not make products float.

Do not rotate products unnaturally.

Do not use fast camera movement.

CAMERA

Mostly static camera.

Optional:
extremely slow push-in;
very subtle parallax;
slow lateral movement of only a few percent.

No zoom effects.
No shaking.
No fast cuts.

LOOP

Duration:
4–6 seconds.

The final frame should connect naturally with the first frame.

Create a seamless loop whenever possible.

PRODUCT PRESENTATION

The product must remain clearly recognizable.

Do not change its shape, material, proportions, logo, packaging or functional details during animation.

If an input product image is provided, preserve the product faithfully.

Do not invent extra buttons, handles, labels or parts.

Do not make the object behave in physically impossible ways.

MOOD

Calm everyday life.

Small comfort.

Order.

Warm home atmosphere.

Thoughtful design.

A beautiful useful thing that naturally belongs in someone's day.

The emotion should be:

«это может пригодиться»

not:

«срочно купите это».

NO TEXT

Do not generate text inside the GIF.

Text will be added separately if needed.

No prices.
No discount stickers.
No sale badges.
No arrows.
No marketplace UI.
No logos unless they already exist on the original product.
No promotional banners.

DO NOT

No flashy advertising aesthetic.
No influencer aesthetic.
No cheap stock-photo aesthetic.
No excessive flowers.
No luxury hotel aesthetic.
No fantasy.
No surrealism.
No impossible physics.
No floating products.
No fast transitions.
No artificial glitter.
No sparkles everywhere.
No dramatic gold effects.
No visual clutter.

OUTPUT GOAL

The result should look like a moving editorial photograph from a calm premium lifestyle magazine.

The viewer should notice the atmosphere first and the product naturally second.
